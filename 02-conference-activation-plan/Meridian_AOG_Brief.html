AOG 2026 — Exhibition Activation Brief

Australasian Oil & Gas Exhibition · Perth Convention & Exhibition Centre · 11–13 March 2026

Executive Summary

This brief sets out Meridian Offshore's commercial and marketing plan for the 2026 Australasian Oil & Gas Exhibition (AOG). AOG is the highest-density audience of WA-based offshore operators, integrity managers, and tier-1 contractors that Meridian engages with all year. Our activation focuses on three outcomes: generating qualified new-business conversations with operators preparing 2026–27 IRM tender cycles, re-engaging existing framework clients through hosted breakfasts, and launching our refreshed subsea inspection capability statement to the broader market.

Scope of Activation

  • 6m × 3m island booth in Hall 4 (target Stand 4–B22).
  • Four-person attending team across commercial, subsea, and operations.
  • Printed capability statement and four service-line one-pagers.
  • Branded 90-second services loop video on booth screen.
  • QR-linked digital brochure for mobile-first audiences.
  • Three hosted breakfasts (15–20 invited guests each) at venue partner.
  • CRM-integrated lead capture with badge-scan workflow.

Commercial Rationale

Three of our five largest existing accounts hold framework agreements expiring within twelve months of the event. AOG offers a low-friction, in-person setting to re-introduce capability and lay the groundwork for the renewal tender cycle.

In parallel, two new-build offshore developments in the Carnarvon and Browse basins are entering the operator-selection phase for inspection scopes in late 2026 — AOG places us in the same room as the relevant integrity managers.
