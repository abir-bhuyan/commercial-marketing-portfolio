## Projects

### [01 — Capability One-Pager](./01-capability-one-pager/)

A single-page client-facing capability statement for a fictional Perth-based subsea integrity company ("Subsea Solutions Pty Ltd"). The deliverable that sits beside a tender response or BD email.

- Header, hero positioning, four-stat strip, services grid, case study, footer
- Brand-consistent editorial layout and typography
- Print-ready A4 PDF, editable HTML source

Deliverable: `SubseaSolutions_Capability.pdf` · Source: `SubseaSolutions_Capability.html`

### [02 — AOG 2026 Conference Activation Plan](./02-conference-activation-plan/)

A 5-slide PowerPoint plus a 1-page supporting brief for a fictional offshore services company ("Meridian Offshore") attending the Australasian Oil & Gas Exhibition (AOG) 2026 in Perth.

- Objective-setting and audience targeting
- Booth concept and key-message development
- Collateral checklist and lead-capture process design
- Indicative budgeting with line-item structure
- Pre-event timeline and post-event success metrics
- Native `.pptx` opens and edits in PowerPoint

Deliverables: `Meridian_AOG_ActivationPlan.pptx` · `Meridian_AOG_Brief.pdf`

### [03 — LinkedIn B2B Campaign — *The Deeper Standard*](./03-linkedin-b2b-campaign/)

An integrated 12-week B2B campaign for Subsea Solutions, designed to build a recognisable point of view on LinkedIn ahead of AOG 2026. Five integrated deliverables:

1. **Campaign Brief** — one-page strategic frame (insight → audience → strategy → KPIs → 3-phase plan)
2. **Brand Mini-Guide + Ads Pack** — logo, four-colour palette, typography hierarchy, tone-of-voice table, plus LinkedIn Sponsored ad mockup and Google Display banner set (728×90, 300×250, 160×600)
3. **12-Week Content Calendar** — colour-coded by phase, with weekly theme, format, copy hook, CTA, hashtag set, and success metric
4. **Email Nurture Sequence** — 4 emails with full mockups, audience triggers, send timing, and target metrics
5. **LinkedIn Post Pack** — 6 designed square tiles with post copy and production notes covering posting cadence, asset format, approval flow, and engagement targets

This is the project that directly demonstrates Canva/digital-marketing-tool fluency — every deliverable is designed in a Canva-equivalent editorial style and can be imported into Canva for further iteration.

### [04 — Marketing Mix Optimiser](./04-marketing-mix-optimiser/)

An offline interactive web app: input a marketing budget, audience, goal, industry, timeline and existing strengths → output a recommended channel mix across twelve B2B channels with projected leads, blended CPL, pipeline ROI, a 90-day roadmap, and a sensitivity view. The single bridge piece in the portfolio — a problem a marketing operations analyst would normally solve in Excel, rebuilt as a self-explaining tool.

- Marketing economics literacy (channel CPL, ramp time, minimum viable spend, diminishing returns)
- Two-pass constrained allocation across competing channels
- Diminishing-returns lead projection (`leads = √(spend / min_spend) × baseline`)
- Auditable output — every channel allocation comes with a generated rationale
- Single self-contained HTML file, no framework, no build step, runs offline

Run with zero commands: open `index.html` in any browser.

## Notes on production

The capability one-pager, the LinkedIn campaign deliverables, and the design portfolio landing page were all designed in HTML and rendered to PDF — the visual output is identical to what would be produced in Canva, and the source HTML is editable for iteration. The PowerPoint deck was generated with `pptxgenjs` to produce a native, editable `.pptx`. The Marketing Mix Optimiser is hand-coded HTML/JavaScript that runs in any browser with no install.

These pieces are portfolio work and reference fictional entities. They are not affiliated with any real company. Industry vocabulary, service descriptions, conference details, and document structures are based on publicly available material from the Australian offshore services sector.
