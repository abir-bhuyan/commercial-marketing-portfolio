# 03 — LinkedIn B2B Campaign · *The Deeper Standard*

A complete 12-week B2B marketing campaign for a fictional Perth subsea integrity services company, designed end-to-end as a portfolio piece for the Fugro Vacation Student — Commercial & Marketing application.

Five integrated deliverables. Designed to look and read like real work a marketing operations team would hand to designers, social leads, and ops.

---

## Why this project exists

The JD asks for someone who can support marketing collateral, conference activity, and client-facing campaigns. The clearest way to demonstrate that capability is not a single artifact — it's an end-to-end campaign showing the four things a marketing analyst actually does:

1. **Strategic framing** — choose an insight, position the audience, set measurable goals
2. **Brand consistency** — define the visual and verbal system the campaign runs on
3. **Editorial planning** — sequence content across weeks and channels with realistic cadence
4. **Conversion thinking** — design how a stranger on LinkedIn becomes a booked meeting at AOG

This project does all four for one fictional client over one quarter.

The campaign is set in Subsea Solutions Pty Ltd's world (same brand as Project 01's capability one-pager), which makes the portfolio feel like one connected piece of marketing thinking — the always-on collateral (P01), the quarterly campaign (P03), and the conference activation it leads into (P02) all work together.

## The campaign in one paragraph

**The Deeper Standard** is a 12-week LinkedIn-led B2B campaign positioning Subsea Solutions as Perth's most accountable subsea integrity partner ahead of FY28 tender season. It runs in three phases — *Establish* (build a point of view), *Deepen* (deliver evidence), *Convert* (turn warm audience into AOG 2026 booth meetings). The campaign's measurable goal is 22 booked meetings and $3.5M+ qualified pipeline.

## What each deliverable actually demonstrates

**Campaign Brief** — Strategic thinking. The brief is structured around the four things a real campaign needs to defend: *the insight* (why this matters), *the audience* (who and where they are), *the strategy* (how recognition becomes conversion), and *the KPIs* (what success looks like, in numbers). No fluff sections like "executive summary" or "background" — every block earns its place.

**Brand Mini-Guide + Ads** — Design system thinking. A one-page brand reference that a small marketing team can apply consistently — logotype rules, four-colour palette with hex/Pantone, typographic hierarchy with live samples, and a tone-of-voice table that contrasts "we sound like" with "we don't sound like" examples. The ads pack on page 2 shows three Display banner sizes (728×90, 300×250, 160×600) and a LinkedIn Sponsored post mock-up, plus a placement strategy note explaining why paid is retargeting-only.

**Content Calendar** — Editorial planning. A landscape two-page calendar with one row per week, colour-coded by phase. Each row has theme, format, copy hook (the actual post text), CTA, hashtag set, and a measurable success metric. The cadence (2× LinkedIn / week, 1× email / fortnight) is realistic for a small team.

**Email Sequence** — Conversion design. Four emails, each with full mockup plus a sidebar showing trigger, audience segment, send time, and target metrics. The sequence escalates from welcome → value → social proof → conversion (book the AOG meeting). The copy is deliberately short, specific, and reply-friendly — modelling what good B2B email actually looks like.

**LinkedIn Post Pack** — Visual design and copy. Six designed square tiles representing different post formats a B2B campaign uses (launch hero, statistic, point-of-view, case study, carousel cover, conference invite). Each tile is paired with its post copy and production notes covering posting cadence, asset format, approval flow, and engagement targets.

## Design system

The campaign uses the same editorial visual system across all five files for consistency:

- **Palette** — Deep Navy (#0d2538) primary container, Signal Coral (#e85d24) accent and CTA, Paper Cream (#faf7f2) background, Steel Grey (#6a7782) metadata
- **Type** — Georgia for display and headlines, Helvetica Neue for body and metadata, all-caps Helvetica for eyebrow labels
- **Layout** — A4 portrait for documents, landscape A4 for the calendar, square (1:1) for LinkedIn tiles
- **Voice** — Specific over general, operator-eye not sales, honest about limits, one thought per sentence

## Honest framing

This is fictional client work. Real things to flag:

- **The KPI targets are illustrative, not benchmarked** against any specific operator's marketing data. They're plausible for a Perth B2B services brand of this size and category, but a real campaign would calibrate against historical performance.
- **The case study, statistics, and quotes are fabricated** for the portfolio. Production work would replace them with the operator's real evidence.
- **The brand "Subsea Solutions Pty Ltd" is fictional** and reused from Project 01 for narrative coherence.
- **The Canva framing** — these files are designed in a Canva-equivalent editorial style and can be imported and edited directly in Canva. They were produced as HTML rendered to PDF rather than in Canva itself, which is disclosed.
